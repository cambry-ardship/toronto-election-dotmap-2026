    Colours are estimates. Each poll's dots match its official totals, rounded to the dot size, but nobody knows which household voted which way. So dots keep swapping colours with others in their poll.

    About the data

    Results: City of Toronto 2026 poll-by-poll results. Advance and mail votes are only reported by ward, so they're spread over each ward's polls in proportion to election-day votes.

    Where dots go: each poll's voters are placed at its residential buildings in proportion to estimated citizens 18+ (2021 Census, apportioned with the StatCan National Address Register, June 2026, and adjusted for buildings occupied since 2021). Buildings are assigned to polls with the City's 2026 street index. Non-voters are not shown.

    Dots sit at residential addresses; buildings with several dots spread them in a spiral. Zoom is capped so individual houses can't be picked out.
